Pricing factors

What affects project pricing?

A custom quote is shaped by the work required to do the project properly, not by a generic package label.

Scope and content

Number of pages, content readiness, page complexity and the amount of structural planning needed.

Functionality

Forms, integrations, animations, CMS needs, filtering, custom interactions and third-party services.

Support needs

Maintenance, content updates, launch support, performance reviews and ongoing improvements.
