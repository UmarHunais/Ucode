Direct Answer • AEO Summary

How much does a website cost in Sri Lanka?

A professional website in Sri Lanka typically costs between LKR 20,000 and LKR 40,000 for a standard 5-page informational business website, LKR 30,000 to LKR 75,000+ for a product catalog or e-commerce storefront, and custom pricing for bespoke web applications requiring advanced databases or dynamic logic.

The final price depends on build architecture (pre-made template vs. hand-coded custom development), page count, functional complexity, on-page SEO preparation, and whether domain registration, hosting, and ongoing maintenance are included. Business owners should compare deliverables and code quality rather than headline figures alone.

The Pricing Confusion

Why Do Website Quotes Vary So Drastically?

If you ask three different Sri Lankan agencies or freelance web designers for a website quotation, you might receive three wildly contrasting figures: LKR 10,000, LKR 40,000, and LKR 350,000+. Why does web design pricing in Sri Lanka vary so drastically?

The difference lies almost entirely in what happens behind the scenes. An agency quoting LKR 10,000 is almost certainly using a pre-made WordPress theme with heavy visual page builders. In contrast, a modern engineering agency writes clean, custom code engineered for speed, conversion, and search rankings. You are comparing two fundamentally different products marketed under the same word: "website."

To evaluate proposals fairly, business owners must look beyond the initial quotation and understand how build architecture, features, and deliverables dictate long-term value. Before signing a contract, reviewing our guide on how to choose a web design agency in Sri Lanka will help you spot common agency red flags.

True Cost of Ownership

Is a Cheap Website Actually Cheaper?

Direct Answer: No. An ultra-cheap website is rarely cheaper over a 12-to-24-month horizon. While the upfront invoice looks appealing, budget websites typically suffer from slow loading times, security vulnerabilities, plugin dependency fees, and high bounce rates that silently drain sales.

When a website takes more than 3 seconds to load on local Sri Lankan mobile connections (Dialog, SLT-Mobitel, Airtel), potential customers abandon the page immediately. As we examine in our article on signs your website is costing you customers, slow page speeds and clunky interfaces directly destroy marketing ROI.

Furthermore, budget template sites frequently break whenever automated plugin updates occur. The business owner inevitably pays for emergency fixes or ends up scrapping the entire site to rebuild from scratch within a year. A well-engineered custom site built on clean code eliminates recurring license costs and delivers dependable business performance for years.

Cost Factors

What Affects the Cost of a Website in Sri Lanka?

Website development costs are driven by five primary architectural and operational factors. Understanding these elements allows you to request accurate, transparent proposals.

1. Build Architecture: Pre-made Template vs. Hand-Coded Custom Development

Pre-built themes and page builders allow non-developers to assemble pages quickly, but they load massive libraries of unused CSS and JavaScript that degrade mobile performance. 2. Page Count & Content Complexity

A focused 5-page informational website requires fewer design iterations and engineering hours than a 20-page corporate portal with dedicated service funnels, case studies, and multilingual support. Providing finalized copywriting, high-resolution photography, and brand assets before project kickoff keeps development timelines and costs strictly within budget.

3. Custom Functionality & Backend Engineering

Standard informational pages are straightforward. However, when your platform requires custom business logic—such as dynamic financial calculators, customer portals, CRM integrations, or a tailored content management system—engineering scope expands. 4. E-Commerce Capabilities & Payment Gateway Integration

Selling products online introduces inventory management, secure user checkout, and payment gateway connectivity. In Sri Lanka, many small businesses begin with catalog-based storefronts featuring WhatsApp direct checkout, which keeps development costs lower (typically LKR 30,000 to LKR 60,000). Full-scale e-commerce stores integrating local payment gateways (e.g., PayHere, WebXPay) or international processors (Stripe) require SSL certification, transaction security, and automated invoice delivery. 5. Ongoing Maintenance & Domain Fees

A website is an active digital asset, not a static brochure. Professional agencies separate domain registration (.lk / .com) and cloud hosting from core development so the client retains legal ownership. Ongoing maintenance (security audits, regular backups, uptime monitoring, and speed checks) in Sri Lanka typically starts from 5,000 LKR/month. Cost Framework

What Is Included in the Price of a Website? One-Time vs. Ongoing Costs

To budget accurately, Sri Lankan businesses must clearly distinguish between one-time development costs and ongoing operational expenses.

One-Time Initial Development

  • UI/UX Design: Custom layouts, typography, and responsive prototypes.
  • Front-End Engineering: Clean, semantic HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript.
  • Mobile Responsiveness: Device-specific testing across smartphones and tablets.
  • Content Integration: Formatting provided text, images, and brand assets.
  • On-Page Technical SEO: Meta tags, heading structure, schema, and sitemaps.
  • Testing & QA: Cross-browser compatibility and launch deployment.

Ongoing Recurring Overhead

  • Domain Name: .lk domains (billed via LK Domain Registry) or .com domains (annual renewal).
  • Web Hosting: Fast cloud SSD hosting or server resources.
  • Maintenance Retainers: Security patches, uptime checks, and backups (from 5,000 LKR/month).
  • Content Updates: Adding new products, portfolio items, or blog posts.
  • Organic SEO Growth: Ongoing search strategy, covered in our SEO services.

Hidden Fees

Beware of Hidden Web Agency Costs

Watch Out for These Unexpected Charges in Sri Lankan Quotes:

  • Recurring Annual Plugin Licensing: Agencies using commercial page builders often pass costly annual software licenses onto unsuspecting clients.
  • Content Upload Penalties: Demanding extra hourly fees simply to format and paste text and images into templates.
  • Domain & Hosting Lock-In: Registering your company domain under the agency's personal account and demanding release fees later. Always ensure you retain primary ownership.
  • Absence of Post-Launch Maintenance: Leaving business owners stranded after launch with no security updates or backup procedures. Learn what is required in our guide to website maintenance after launch.
Evaluation Checklist

How to Compare Website Quotes in Sri Lanka: A Business Checklist

When evaluating proposals from multiple web design companies or freelancers, compare these 8 crucial criteria line-by-line before making a financial commitment:

  1. Code Architecture: Is the website hand-coded using clean HTML/CSS/JS, or built on a heavy pre-made WordPress theme builder?
  2. Explicit Page Deliverables: Does the quote specify the exact number of pages, custom layouts, and contact forms included?
  3. Mobile Responsiveness: Is mobile optimization guaranteed across all screen sizes and tested on real mobile browsers?
  4. Speed & Core Web Vitals: Does the agency commit to performance benchmarks so the site doesn't load sluggishly on mobile networks?
  5. On-Page Technical SEO: Are title tags, meta descriptions, and Google-friendly schema markup included in the baseline scope?
  6. Revision Policy: How many rounds of design revisions are permitted before extra design charges kick in?
  7. Legal Ownership: Will you receive full ownership of the source code, domain name, and hosting credentials upon final payment?
  8. Post-Launch Support: Does the agency include a warranty window or provide an affordable monthly maintenance package?

FAQ • Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Website Costs

Direct, factual answers to common questions about website pricing in Sri Lanka.

How much does a website cost in Sri Lanka?

A standard 5-page informational business website in Sri Lanka typically ranges from LKR 20,000 to LKR 40,000. E-commerce and catalog websites range from LKR 30,000 to LKR 75,000+, while custom web applications with dynamic databases or calculators receive custom scopes based on engineering complexity.

Why do website quotes vary so drastically in Sri Lanka?

Quotes vary widely because agencies provide different technical solutions under the same label. Low quotes (LKR 10,000) usually rely on pre-made WordPress templates with bloated plugins, whereas professional agencies write clean, hand-coded HTML/CSS/JS tailored for speed, SEO, and long-term security.

What is the cheapest way to build a business website?

The cheapest initial option is a template builder or low-cost freelancer, but this often incurs recurring plugin licenses, slower load times, and rebuild costs. An honest, streamlined custom basic package (such as UCODE's LKR 20,000 tier) provides clean hand-coded performance with minimal ongoing overhead.

How much does an e-commerce website cost in Sri Lanka?

An e-commerce website with product catalog displays, WhatsApp order funnels, and standard mobile checkout typically ranges from LKR 30,000 to LKR 75,000. Advanced platforms requiring full shopping carts, payment gateways (PayHere, WebXPay), and inventory management receive custom quotes.

Are domain names and web hosting included in website packages?

Professional web agencies generally bill domain registration (e.g., .lk or .com) and cloud hosting separately from design and development. This ensures the business owner retains full legal ownership and direct control of their digital domain and hosting accounts.

How much does website maintenance cost after launch?

Routine website maintenance in Sri Lanka typically starts from LKR 5,000 per month. This covers security monitoring, regular backups, software and performance audits, minor content updates, and server health checks.

Why are custom-coded websites more expensive than templates?

Custom-coded websites require dedicated software engineering, custom UI/UX design, and manual performance optimization rather than drag-and-drop themes. In return, they deliver faster load speeds, higher Google rankings, zero plugin bloat, and complete ownership.

How long does it take to build a website in Sri Lanka?

A standard 5-page informational business website or product catalog typically takes 2 to 3 weeks to design, code, test, and launch when content is prepared. Complex custom platforms or multi-functional systems require custom project milestone schedules.
