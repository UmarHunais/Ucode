The Pricing Confusion

Why Web Design Quotes Vary So Drastically

If you ask three different Sri Lankan agencies for a website quotation, you might receive three completely different figures: LKR 10,000, LKR 40,000, and LKR 350,000. Why does web development pricing vary so widely?

The difference lies under the hood. A LKR 10,000 website is almost always built by slapping text onto a pre-made WordPress theme using heavy drag-and-drop page builders. Conversely, a high-performance web agency writes clean, custom code tailored to your exact business goals.

Cost Factors

The 4 Major Factors That Drive Website Costs

1. Build Architecture: Template vs. Custom Code

Templates are cheap upfront but expensive over time. Pre-built themes force your site to load thousands of lines of unused CSS and JavaScript, slowing down page speed on local 4G connections. Custom hand-coded builds (semantic HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript) load instantly and rank higher in search engines. You can review UCODE's transparent package tiers to see how honest scope pricing is structured.

2. Page Count & Content Complexity

Building a clean 5-page informational website requires far fewer design and engineering hours than a 20-page corporate portal with custom product displays. Having your copywriting, images, and brand assets organized before project kickoff keeps development timelines and costs predictable.

3. Custom Functionality & Backend Engineering

Standard marketing pages are straightforward. However, if your site requires custom features—such as dynamic ROI calculators, customer tracking portals, payment gateway integrations, or custom CMS admin dashboards—engineering complexity increases. For instance, when UCODE developed Natures Treasure's custom platform, we built a bespoke ROI yield calculator and order management backend tailored specifically to Agarwood tree investors.

4. Ongoing Maintenance & Domain Fees

A website is an active asset, not a static poster. Professional agencies separate domain registration (.lk / .com) and database server hosting from the core development scope. Hidden Fees

Beware of Hidden Web Agency Costs

Watch Out for These Unexpected Charges:

  • Recurring Annual Theme/Plugin Licensing: Agencies using commercial plugins often pass monthly SaaS fees onto clients.
  • Content Upload Penalties: Charging extra fees simply to paste provided text into page layouts.
  • Domain Lock-In Fees: Demanding high fees to transfer domain ownership back to your business.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common website pricing questions in Sri Lanka.

How much does a professional website cost in Sri Lanka in 2026?

A standard 5-page informational business website typically ranges from LKR 20,000 to LKR 40,000. E-commerce platforms and product displays range from LKR 30,000 to LKR 75,000+. Custom web applications with specialized database integrations and ROI systems receive custom scopes based on complexity.

Why do web design prices vary so drastically in Sri Lanka?

Price variations depend heavily on build architecture (pre-made WordPress template vs. hand-coded custom development), page count, backend functionality, SEO preparation, and whether domain hosting and ongoing maintenance are included.

Are domain names and web hosting included in website packages?

Professional web agencies usually bill domain registration (e.g. .lk or .com) and database hosting separately from the core design and development scope to ensure the client maintains direct legal ownership of their digital assets.
